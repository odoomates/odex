# odeX privacy policy

odeX does not collect, transmit or sell any data.

- It runs only when you click its toolbar button or press its shortcut, and only in that tab.
- It talks only to the Odoo server of the page you opened it on, with your existing session, so
  every call runs as your Odoo user, with your access rights.
- The results of your calls are shown in the panel and kept in memory only; they are never stored
  or sent anywhere.
- Stored locally in your browser with `storage.local`: your settings, the panel's size and position,
  the code you are typing (so it survives a reload), and a history of up to 200 calls you ran
  (the code, the Odoo address and database name, whether it succeeded, a short summary such as
  "3 items", and how long it took). **Clear history** in the history panel deletes it.
- There is no analytics, telemetry, remote code or third-party service.
